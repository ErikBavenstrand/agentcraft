# Key roles: rotation first, one muscle memory for every class

The owner is an altoholic (2026-11-23) and wants every spec's real rotation on the best buttons (2026-11-26: "the rotations for ALL specs are basically ignored... ensure the layout on keyboard AND controller is optimal for that. Pay extra attention to controller layout"; tanks must tank and level, healers must heal, from both devices).

Two levels:
- Hard rule: the keybinds are identical for every class: the same 12 keys on Bar 1, Shift on Bar 2, Ctrl on Bar 4, Alt on Bar 3, the same pet keys and the same controller buttons. Keybinds.lua is class-independent; never add class-specific binds.
- Placement rule (rotation first): each spec's rotation is written down as a priority list from the guides (the comment block at the top of each Layouts/<Class>.lua, with sources), and its buttons go on the best keys in press-frequency order, using the slots below. "Rotation slot 1" is the button you press most in that spec, on every class. The same KIND of ability lands on the same key across classes because the slots are ordered by how you play, not by spell type.
  Every key placement says why in the layout's comments. Every class and spec in one table each: `dev/class-layouts.md`, generated by `luajit dev/class-layouts.lua > dev/class-layouts.md`.

## What counts as the rotation

- Damage specs: everything in the single-target priority list, plus the AoE button, the interrupt and the gap closer or opener.
- Tanks: the threat priority list (single target and multi-target), the taunt, the interrupt, the AoE threat button and the go-to mitigation button. Leveling as a tank uses the same buttons: no separate "leveling" bar.
- Healers: main efficient heal, fast heal, big heal, HoT or shield, dispel, group heal; damage for solo/leveling goes one tier down (Shift / LT+RT), except a healer spec whose leveling plays like damage (Shadow, Balance, Elemental) - that's a damage spec.
- Everything else (buffs, cooldowns, CC, utility) goes after the rotation, by how often and how urgently it's pressed.

## Controller page 1 (Steam Deck, Gamepad UI)

On a pad the left thumb steers with the stick, so a D-pad press stops your movement; the right thumb is free for face buttons while a trigger is held. So the rotation lives on face buttons, and the D-pad takes what you press standing still, before a pull, or rarely.
Holding one trigger is easy; holding both is a stretch; RT is the default trigger for your attacks (the Gamepad UI's stance bar replaces it in a stance, and Agentcraft mirrors it there, so it never changes under you).

## Consumables: why these keys

The item macros (owner decision, 2026-11-24) are generated from your bags (`Macros/Items.lua`), so one macro per kind of consumable works for every class:
- Same keys everywhere: an altoholic presses Alt+Q for a heal potion on every character, and LT X on the pad. No class file may move them [rules: layout-shared, and the Alt bar is never read from a class].
- Alt+Q E R T sit under the main hand, like Q E R T, and read top-down in urgency: heal (Q, as Shift+Q is the self-heal spell), mana (E), bandage (R, out of danger), food (T, out of combat). Fish and Bait stay on F and G, trinkets on C and V, the Hearthstone on 4.
- Controller: LT is the item layer of page 2 (Bait A, trinkets Up/Down), so the consumables take its free face buttons in the same order (HealPot X, ManaPot Y, Bandage B) and Food LT Left. Mage and druid page 2 spells that sat there moved to page 3.
- Class consumables fold into the shared macros instead of getting their own: a warlock's Healthstone is HealPot's first choice (free, its own cooldown; once used the macro moves on to potions), a mage's mana gem is ManaPot's first choice for the same reason, and conjured food wins its tier in Food. Warriors and rogues have no mana: ManaPot isn't offered and Alt+E / LT Y stay empty ("your class has no mana") rather than holding something else, so the key means the same thing on every character. Hunter pet food is not player food: raw meat and fish are never in the Food list (Feed Pet keeps its own home). Drinks are not handled.
